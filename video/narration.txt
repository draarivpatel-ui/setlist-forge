SetlistForge is a cross-domain music, film, and cultural taste setlist sequencer built for the Qloo Global Hackathon.
Select seeds across films, books, venues, and music tracks to compute an eight-dimensional taste centroid and inspect the cultural bridge explaining why each track resonates with your non-music seeds.
Next, choose an energy arc curve and run the beam-search sequencer, which balances seed affinity, target energy progression, and Camelot wheel harmonic key transitions across the entire setlist.
You can audition any harmonic transition in the browser with the built-in Web Audio synthesizer, connect a live Qloo Insights API key, and export the finished setlist as M3U8, JSON, or liner notes.
